# Manufacturing Referral-Partner Prospects — Mid-Atlantic

**Purpose:** Small, traditional consultants who already serve SMB manufacturers in PA/NJ/MD/DE
and have owner trust + a client channel, but **no in-house AI/software capability** — i.e.,
people whose clients ask them for "AI stuff" they can't deliver. These are *referral /
collaboration partners*, not clients and not competitors.

**Commissioned by:** Kenius — solo AI-automation consultant, Allentown PA (ex-EY ML engineer).
Builds back-office workflow automation for 20–150-employee manufacturers: RFQ/PO/cert data
extraction, auto-quoting, compliance & mill-cert package assembly, service reports/invoices,
ERP↔accounting↔CRM↔WMS glue.

**Method:** Six parallel research passes (one per partner category), every firm verified against
a live web page. Firms that already sell AI/automation, run a real bench, or sit outside the
region are listed under *Disqualified* so they aren't re-chased. Ranked strongest-fit first, per
the thesis that **systems-touching + compliance-paperwork consultants who can't automate are the
best partners.** Overlaid with a second lens: *how fast could this person actually send a warm
referral* (proximity + founder-reachability + thematic overlap).

> ⚠️ Cross-validation note: **Prosit (EJ Lydon)** was surfaced independently by 3 of the 6
> research passes. Everything else came from a single pass. Treat single-source firms as
> "verified exists + looks right," not "vetted" — confirm size/fit on the first call.

## CHANNELS & ECOSYSTEM (not partners themselves — routes to more partners)

The single biggest tactical finding: **true micro-firm ERP and ops consultants are sparse on the open
web** — the SMB-manufacturing ERP niche (E2/JobBOSS/Global Shop/Made2Manage) is largely serviced by the
software vendors' own people or by solo practitioners who never build a marketing site. Web search alone
under-samples this category. Better veins:

1. **MEP affiliate rosters.** Don't pitch the centers as partners — ask each for its roster of
   *independent affiliated/subcontracted consultants* (finance/ops/lean/quality advisors they bring in).
   Those solo advisors match the target profile far better than the centers. Closest to you: **MRC
   (Bethlehem)** and **MANTEC (York)**. You already have an MRC connection — use it for this list.
2. **SimplyQuality referral network** surfaced several solo QMS people worth a direct LinkedIn look:
   Charles Scalies (Source4Quality, PA), Charlie Facas (Pompton Lakes NJ), Genevieve Lemaire (PA).
3. **LinkedIn people-search**, not company-search, for ERP: query strings like *"independent Epicor
   consultant Pennsylvania"*, *"JobBOSS implementation New Jersey"*, *"E2 Shoptech consultant"*,
   *"Global Shop Solutions consultant PA"*. Individuals surface where firms don't.

## What this list is — and isn't

This is a **channel**, not a buyer. Warm referral is your fastest path in, and it's worth building — but
a finished partner list can quietly become the reason a manufacturer call slips another week. The list is
ranked so the top five cost you a phone call, not a research project. Outreach drafts for Tier 1 are in
`partner-outreach-drafts.md`. Send one before you add a name.
