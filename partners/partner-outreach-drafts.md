# Tier-1 Partner Outreach — Drafts

**Frame for all of these:** You are *not* selling. You're offering to be the specialist they can bring
in or refer to when their clients ask for "AI stuff" — so the work leaves the room as a referral instead
of a request they have to turn down. Position: complementary, not competitive. Lead with *their* client's
pain, not your capability. Keep it short. Offer a referral fee or reciprocal referrals explicitly — it
removes the "what's the catch" and makes saying yes easy.

**Channel:** For most of these, a short LinkedIn connection note or a plain email beats a form. For
Gallagher (Allentown) and Compass Point (Bethlehem), a coffee is the real move — the message just books it.
