Asked before, answered straight

Fair questions.

Do you sell a product, or build custom?

Custom, always. We’re a consultancy: we study how you work, write the plan, and build to it — into the tools you already use. No licenses to a platform of ours, no rip-and-replace.

Where do we start if we’re not sure AI fits?

With the plan. One to two weeks, fixed fee: we map where your hours go and hand you a costed recommendation — even if the honest recommendation is “not yet.” The plan is yours either way.

Is our data safe?

Yours stays yours: never sold, never used to train public models, and agents get scoped access to only the systems the job needs — with a human approval step before anything consequential.

What does it cost?

Fixed fee per phase, quoted up front — the plan first, then the pilot (typically four to eight weeks). You always know the number before we start.

The next step

Tell us what eats the day.

Thirty minutes, no slides. We’ll tell you honestly whether AI can help — and exactly what it would cost.
